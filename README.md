# The Tiny Town

Cosy isometric AI-driven village life simulation built with Unity 6 and local Ollama AI.

- Design: [GameDesignDocument.md](GameDesignDocument.md)
- Building, testing and downloading Windows builds: [Documentation/BuildAndTest.md](Documentation/BuildAndTest.md)

Status: Phase 2. One resident, Mabel, lives her day in a small isometric village with placeholder art. She has eight needs, a job at the community garden, meals, sleep and free time. When a local Ollama service is running, she chooses how to spend her free time through the AI (the game checks every choice), says short lines and reflects on each day at bedtime. She keeps memories, and the town saves them. Without Ollama she follows her built-in routine. Controls: Space pauses, keys 1–4 set the speed, F5 saves, F9 loads, Tab switches map and F follows Mabel.
